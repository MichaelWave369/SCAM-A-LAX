# Security and responsible-use policy

SCAM-A-LAX is defensive software.

## In scope

- Organizing reports and case notes.
- Hashing user-supplied evidence locally.
- Flagging scam indicators in text supplied by the user.
- Producing evidence manifests and case packets.
- Victim-safety guidance and awareness training.

## Out of scope

Do not use this project to create or distribute malware, steal credentials,
intercept communications without authorization, gain unauthorized access,
retaliate against suspected scammers, dox people, or interfere with systems
you do not own or have explicit permission to test.

A suspicion, model output, heuristic score, or correlation is not proof of
identity or wrongdoing. Human review and lawful process remain required.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please open a GitHub issue containing a minimal reproduction without secrets,
private victim data, credentials, or live malicious payloads. For sensitive
reports, use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting feature when available.
