# Handoff Profiles

SCAM-A-LAX handoff profiles are presentation filters over one case ledger. They do not create a second truth source and they never promote derived intelligence into evidence.

## Victim Copy

Readable case/timeline evidence summary for the victim or a trusted helper. Analyst-only links are omitted. The packet explicitly states that it is not legal advice or a finding of wrongdoing.

## Bank / Fraud Department

Emphasizes payment, wallet, communications, remote-access context, URLs, files, and relevant identifiers that may help a fraud team review a reported incident.

## Platform Abuse Report

Emphasizes messages, account/contact indicators, URLs/domains, remote-access references, and source metadata. Internal analyst notes are omitted from the case summary.

## Law-Enforcement / Investigator Packet

Includes the full evidence selection plus the non-authoritative entity index, cross-case correlations, and explicitly state-labeled analyst links.

## Shared guarantees

- Evidence records keep their original IDs, timestamps, states, and hashes.
- Extracted entities remain labeled `NON_AUTHORITATIVE`.
- Correlation does not imply common ownership, identity, intent, or guilt.
- Exports do not contain original file bytes.
- The user should review the packet before sharing it with any third party.
